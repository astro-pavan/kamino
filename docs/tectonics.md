# Crust Composition

The oceanic crust composition is calculated as a function of the mantle potential temperature and the Mg-Si ratio. The oxide composition at an Mg-Si ratio of 1 is interpolated from a range of different compositions at different mantle potential temperatures from Herzberg et al. (2007). The Mg and Si are then exhanged depending on the Mg-Si ratio. These oxide compositions are then turned into a mineral assmeblage using the CIPW norm. 

# Outgassing

Carbon and chlorine enter into the ocean via outgassing. This is assumed to be at a constant rate. The cholrine is assumed to enter the ocean as HCl and thus adds a unit of alkalinity per unit of cholrine.
